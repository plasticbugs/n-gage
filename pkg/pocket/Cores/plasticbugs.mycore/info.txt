My Core
MANUFACTURER, YEAR

One paragraph on what the game is and
what is in the gateware. Plain
characters only, 32 lines at most,
* for bullets.

ROMs are not included. Build the image
from your own MAME mycore romset with
mra_build.py and mycore.mra, and copy
it to Assets/mycore/common/mycore.rom

* A / Y  button 1
* B / X  button 2
* Select coin, Start start

Analogizer: CRT video and SNAC pads,
set in this core's menu (Analogizer:
On; H and V Position centre the
picture). The slot is powered: remove
any cartridge first.
