        Read it instead
        1. Want to play your N-Gage games on your Analogue Pocket? It’s easy. Let’s go!
        2. First, take the little memory card out of your Pocket, and put it in your computer.
        3. On this page, press Choose my SD card. Find your card, click its folder called Assets, and press Select Folder. If the computer asks whether that’s okay, say yes.
        4. Now grab your game zips. As many as you like! Drop them all onto this page at once.
        5. Each game turns into a card the Pocket can read, and goes straight onto your SD card, with its name in the Pocket’s menu. A green tick means it’s done.
        6. One more thing. The Pocket also needs the N-Gage’s own software, a file called SYM dot ROM. Drop it on the page once, and you’re set.
        7. Last step. Tell your computer you’re done with the card, that’s called ejecting, and put it back in your Pocket. Open openFPGA, then N-Gage, and pick a game.
        8. The screen stays dark for a little while as the game loads. Then, hello Nokia! Have fun.

        If something isn’t right

        Where do I get SYM.ROM?

        It’s the N-Gage’s own software, so it isn’t included. Copy it from your own N-Gage, or find bios/Nokia/N-Gage/SYM.ROM in the retrobios collection on GitHub.

        The right one is 18,612,224 bytes. Drop it in step 2 and this page checks it for you, then names it sym.rom on the card.

        A game won’t go on the card

        The Pocket’s game card holds 32 MB, so Glimmerati and Pathway to Glory are too big.

        This page opens .zip files and folders. For a .7z or .rar, right-click it and extract it first, then drop the folder you get.

        A game won’t start

        Asphalt Urban GT 2, Warhammer 40,000: Glory in Death and One check the memory card they were put on, so their copy protection stops them here. N-Gage 2.0 games don’t run either.

        If the screen stays dark for more than two minutes, take the card out and check that sym.rom is in Assets\ngage\common.

        Does this page send my games anywhere?

        No. It all happens on this computer, inside this page, and it works with no internet.
